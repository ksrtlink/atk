# Personal accounts — 16 October 2026

A verified email identity automatically opens one private personal workspace, with no company request or approval required. Existing company users retain access to their assigned company and can select Personal or Company as a library context. Ordinary accounts still cannot choose another company. Personal workspace IDs and ownership derive from the verified Firebase UID, never submitted account fields.

Migration `0005_personal.sql` adds workspace kind and immutable personal owner identity. Existing workspaces remain companies. A unique index allows one personal workspace per user; database triggers reject personal team membership and ownership/kind reassignment. Private API authorization accepts only the caller's personal workspace or authorized company context. Platform-admin status does not grant access to other people's personal libraries. Public short links and QR redirect URLs remain public for sharing; their editable library and reporting remain private.

Personal pilot limits are enforced on the server: 25 links, 25 saved QR designs, 25 tracked QR identities, 10 rows per bulk batch and 10 saved bulk jobs. Company limits remain unchanged. The existing renderer supports SVG, PNG and JPG plus bulk ZIP/PDF. Analytics stays opt-in per workspace with the confirmed 90-day policy. No extra paid service or plan upgrade was introduced; provider usage limits still apply.

Company approval and invitations remain separate from personal signup. A company join never moves personal links, QR designs, presets, tracking or analytics. The existing explicit-confirmation import handles only old unassigned records; it can import into the private library or share into the assigned company with clear confirmation. Moving newly saved personal workspace content to a company is not offered by this release, so it cannot happen implicitly.

Validation: 31 backend tests pass, including personal ownership, admin/other-user denial, no personal invitations/team members, immutable ownership, separate records after joining, and saved/trackable/bulk limits. Enterprise browser checks cover personal link creation without approval, personal QR saving, switching to the assigned company without leaking the private design, company workflows and desktop/mobile layouts. Analytics/QR export browser regressions also pass. Firebase responses are simulated with signed test tokens; actual email delivery was not independently tested.

Before migration, a private production backup restored successfully into independent SQLite: 15 tables, 12 records, SHA-256 d2b0e11eff0e587ed2aa81876cedd665525387c53f2a56f0dc7d9256f3ea1289. Staging migration/deployment passed before production migration. The original eight links were preserved, and production foreign-key validation returned no violations. Backup data remains in ignored local QA storage.

Rollback: preserve all issued redirect routes and database columns. Prefer a corrected forward deployment. Old versions do not expose personal workspace libraries, so do not describe reverting to a company-only UI as a complete functional rollback. Never convert personal workspaces to companies or restore over newly created user data as a routine rollback.
