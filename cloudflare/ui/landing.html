A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Before your first scan.

Who is TinyTrail for?

Companies creating links and QR codes across campaigns, reports, events, and printed materials. The pilot uses approved company workspaces rather than individual personal libraries.

Can I create QR codes without short links?

Yes. Company members can encode a direct website URL, style the code, and save it to the shared library. A direct QR opens that exact URL; only QR codes using a TinyTrail short link can have their destination updated through TinyTrail.

What can I create in bulk?

Import up to 100 rows per batch to create short links, direct QR designs, or both. Export QR files in a ZIP, download a results CSV, and generate six-code PDF sheets.

How does company access work?

Request a pilot workspace using a verified email. Once approved, the company owner can invite colleagues with email-bound links that expire after seven days.

Does TinyTrail verify destination websites?

Company membership controls who can create and edit links. TinyTrail does not currently scan destination websites for malware or certify their content.
