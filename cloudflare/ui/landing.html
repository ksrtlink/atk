A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Before your first scan.

Who is TinyTrail for?

Individuals and companies creating links and QR codes for reports, events, and printed materials. A verified email opens your private library; shared company workspaces require approval or an invitation.

Can I create QR codes without short links?

Yes. Anyone with a verified account can style a QR code for a website without creating a short link. Static codes keep the encoded URL; tracked TinyTrail QR codes keep a stable URL with an editable destination.

What can I create in bulk?

Import up to 10 rows on a personal account, or 100 in a company workspace, to create short links, direct QR designs, or both. Export QR files in a ZIP, download a results CSV, and generate six-code PDF sheets.

How does company access work?

Request a pilot workspace using a verified email. Once approved, the company owner can invite colleagues with email-bound links that expire after seven days.

Does TinyTrail verify destination websites?

Company membership controls who can create and edit links. TinyTrail does not currently scan destination websites for malware or certify their content.
